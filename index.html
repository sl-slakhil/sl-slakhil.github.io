About

I am a doctoral researcher in the Structural Dynamics & Vibration Laboratory at IIST, Thiruvananthapuram, working with Dr. Praveen Krishna I R. My thesis is titled “Meshfree Method for Static and Dynamic Analysis of One-Dimensional Nonlocal Elastic Structures.”

Classical continuum models miss the size effects seen in nanostructures. Nonlocal elasticity can capture them, but it brings integral kernels and extra constitutive boundary conditions that make numerical solution hard. I build Element-Free Galerkin formulations that handle these problems accurately for rods, Euler–Bernoulli and Timoshenko beams. I check them against exact solutions and atomistic results.

Next, I want to take these ideas to particle methods (EFG, SPH and related schemes) for multi-dimensional solids, large deformation and fluid–structure interaction.

Research

Themes I work on, roughly from core expertise to current extensions.

Element-Free Galerkin for nonlocal elasticity

MLS-based EFG formulations for Eringen strain-driven (differential and integral) and Romano–Barretta stress-driven models. I study how the non-dimensional length scale, support size and nonlocal parameter affect accuracy.

  • MLS shape functions
  • Rods
  • EB & Timoshenko beams

Constitutive boundary conditions

Stress-driven models need constitutive boundary conditions that most methods only satisfy approximately. I develop exact boundary treatments and generalized stress-driven EFG schemes for nanorods and nanobeams.

  • Lagrange multipliers
  • Scaled transformation
  • Exact CBCs

Nonlocal elastodynamics

Free and forced vibration, buckling and transient wave propagation in nanobeams and nanorods. I use modal superposition with Newmark-β time integration and non-dimensional dispersion analysis.

  • Vibration
  • Buckling
  • Dispersion

Particle methods: SPH & TL-SPH current

I am extending my meshfree background to Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics. This includes total-Lagrangian SPH for beams, studying shear locking and stability, and a C++ fluid–structure interaction code for HPC clusters.

  • TL-SPH
  • FSI
  • C++ / HPC

Atomistic–continuum validation

I compare nonlocal continuum predictions with atomistic models: force-constant models of carbon nanotubes and molecular dynamics of nanowires. The goal is to check that the models reproduce size-dependent stiffening.

  • CNTs
  • Molecular dynamics
  • Size effects

Stress-driven finite elements

Displacement-based and two-field mixed FE formulations for stress-driven nonlocal beams and 2D/3D solids. I look at checkerboard instabilities in equal-order interpolation and stabilize them.

  • Mixed FEM
  • Stabilization
  • 2D / 3D solids
